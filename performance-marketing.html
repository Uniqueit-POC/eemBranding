Questions & Answers

Performance marketing is a digital marketing approach where campaigns are optimised for measurable results like clicks, leads, and sales instead of just visibility.

Performance marketing focuses on data and real-time optimisation, allowing businesses to spend on strategies that generate higher conversions and better returns.

Common channels include Google Ads (search), social media ads, display advertising, remarketing, and other paid digital platforms.

CRO improves how users interact with a website, helping convert more visitors into customers without increasing ad spend.

Remarketing targets users who have already visited a website, reminding them about the brand and increasing the chances of conversion.

Success is measured using metrics like cost per lead, return on ad spend (ROAS), conversions, and overall campaign performance.
